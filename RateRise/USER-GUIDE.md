# RateRise — Owner's User Guide (Plain English)

This guide assumes zero technical background. It explains what RateRise is, how to open it, and
how to use every screen — both for pricing your own business and for selling the tool itself.

---

## What is RateRise, in one paragraph?

RateRise is a single file (`index.html`) that opens in any web browser — Chrome, Safari, Edge,
Firefox, on a computer or a phone. It is not a website you log into and not an app you install.
Double-click the file (or open the hosted link) and it just works, even without internet.
Everything you type is saved automatically on that device, so when you come back, your numbers
are still there. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## How to open it

- **On a computer:** double-click `index.html`. It opens in your browser. That's it.
- **From the hosted link:** just visit the link. To keep a copy, use the browser's
  *Save Page As → Webpage, HTML Only*.
- **On a phone:** open the hosted link, or email yourself the file and open it.

There is nothing to install, no account, no password, no subscription.

## The five tabs, screen by screen

### 1. Home
The welcome page. It explains the tool and has the **free cheat-sheet email form** at the bottom
— that form is for *your future customers*, not for you (see "Selling it" below).

### 2. Rate Calculator — "What should I charge?"
Fill in five numbers:
1. **Desired annual take-home income** — what you want to personally earn in a year.
2. **Annual business expenses** — software, ads, supplies, fees.
3. **Tax set-aside %** — the slice you must save for taxes (25–30% is typical for self-employed).
4. **Weeks you work per year** — 52 minus vacation weeks.
5. **Billable hours per week** — hours a client actually pays for. Be honest: admin, marketing,
   and emails are NOT billable. Most solo businesses can bill 15–25 hours a week, not 40.

The big pink number is your **minimum hourly rate** — the floor that makes your income goal
actually happen. You also get a day rate, a minimum project price, and (if you enter your current
rate) a warning showing how much money you're losing per year by undercharging.

**Use it when:** setting prices for the first time, raising rates, or sanity-checking a project quote.

### 3. Package Builder — "How do I sell it?"
Clients say yes to packages more easily than to hourly rates. Here you:
1. Type each service you offer and what it **costs you to deliver** (your hours × your new rate,
   plus materials).
2. Assign each service to a tier: **Essentials** (basic), **Signature** (your main offer), or
   **Premium** (the works). Higher tiers automatically include everything below them.
3. Set a **profit margin** (55% is a healthy default).

The three pricing cards on the right update instantly — those are the packages you put on your
website or say out loud on a sales call. The middle "Signature" tier is highlighted on purpose:
most buyers pick the middle option.

### 4. Quote Generator — "Send something professional"
Fill in your business name, the client's name, and your line items (use your package prices!).
Add a tax rate and a deposit percentage if you want. The polished quote builds itself on the
right as you type. Click **Print / Save quote as PDF**, choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog,
and email it to your client. A branded PDF quote signals "professional" — and professionals get
paid more.

### 5. Goal Tracker — "Am I on track this month?"
Enter your monthly revenue goal and your average sale value. It tells you exactly how many
clients you need this month and per week. Each time you land a sale, log it under "Log a win" —
the progress bar fills up. Small psychological trick, big motivation.

### Launch & Earn — "Make money from the tool itself"
This tab is the business-in-a-box:
- The **two business models** (sell it as a download, or give it away to collect leads and sell
  your bigger service).
- A **5-step launch checklist**.
- The **checkout button setup** — paste your own store link so the buy button works.
- The **lead manager** — every email captured by the Home-page form appears here. You can export
  them all as a CSV file (opens in Excel/Google Sheets) or auto-forward them to your inbox by
  pasting a free form endpoint (explained in DEPLOYMENT.md).

## Common questions

**I closed the browser — is my data gone?** No. It's saved on your device automatically and will
be there when you reopen the same file in the same browser.

**Can I use it on two devices?** Yes, but each device keeps its own data (nothing syncs, because
nothing is uploaded — that's the privacy feature).

**Can I change the name and colors?** Yes — see the "Rebrand it" section in README.md. It's a
two-minute edit in any text editor.

**Do I need to keep paying for anything to keep using it?** No. No hosting, no subscription, no
AI, no account. The file is yours forever.
