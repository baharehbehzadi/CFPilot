# RateRise — How to Sell It & Make Money (Step-by-Step Roadmap)

A practical, no-fluff plan to take RateRise from a file on your computer to a product that earns.
You don't need an audience, a website, or tech skills to start. Follow it in order.

---

## The two business models (pick one to start)

### Model A — Sell it as a digital product (fastest to first dollar)
Sell the app as a one-time download. Zero delivery cost, no customer support headaches, it sells
while you sleep. **Best if you want passive income.**

### Model B — Free lead magnet + high-ticket upsell (highest earnings)
Give the tool away free to collect emails, then sell your real offer — a pricing audit, coaching,
or a done-for-you package — to people who just proved to themselves they're underpricing.
**Best if you already sell a service.** The app has a built-in email-capture form and a lead
manager (Launch & Earn tab) made exactly for this.

You can run both at once: sell the tool cheap AND upsell your service to buyers.

---

## Phase 1 — Set it up (Day 1, ~1 hour)

1. **Brand it.** Open `index.html`. Change "RateRise" to your business name in the header and page
   title. Change the accent color by editing the `--brand` and `--brand-2` values near the top of
   the `<style>` block.
2. **Create a free store.** Sign up at **Gumroad** or **Payhip** (both free, both handle checkout
   and take payment for you). Etsy also works and has built-in traffic (small listing fee).
3. **Upload the file** as the digital download. Set your price ($19 launch price is a good start).
4. **Write the listing** — paste from `LISTING.md`, which is already written to sell.
5. **Set your checkout link** inside the app: go to the **Launch & Earn** tab, paste your Gumroad/
   Payhip link and price. The buy button then works everywhere you share the file.
6. **(Model B only)** Paste a free form endpoint (Formspree/Getform/ConvertKit) into the "Email
   form endpoint" field on the Launch & Earn tab so captured emails land in your inbox.

## Phase 2 — First 5 sales (Week 1)

Your first sales come from people who already know you. That's normal and good.

1. **Post the problem, not the product.** On Instagram, TikTok, Threads, or Facebook, share the
   hook: *"Most [photographers/coaches/VAs] are undercharging by $20k+ a year and don't know it.
   I built a 5-minute tool that shows you your real number."* Link your store.
2. **DM 10 peers** in your field: "I made a little pricing tool — want the link?" Ask for honest
   feedback and a review.
3. **Offer a launch discount** for 72 hours to create urgency ("$19 this week, then $37").
4. **Collect 2–3 testimonials** from early buyers. Add them to your listing. Social proof is what
   converts strangers.

## Phase 3 — Steady sales (Weeks 2–4)

1. **Make content a habit.** 3 short posts/week, each teaching ONE pricing idea (e.g. "Why you
   should never charge hourly," "The Good-Better-Best trick," "How to raise rates without losing
   clients"). End each with a soft link to the tool.
2. **Use Pinterest** if your audience is there (coaches, weddings, creatives, handmade) — pin
   graphics that link to your listing. Pinterest traffic compounds for months.
3. **Start an email list.** Every buyer and every free-cheat-sheet lead goes on it. Email them a
   tip a week and occasionally your paid offers.
4. **Bundle to raise price.** Add a template pack, a pricing-scripts PDF ("what to say when a
   client asks for a discount"), or a 10-minute walkthrough video. Sell the bundle for $49–$67.

## Phase 4 — Scale it (Month 2+)

1. **Add an upsell** at checkout (Gumroad supports this): a $99 "Pricing Power Hour" call or a
   deeper course.
2. **Affiliates / partners.** Let other creators sell it for a cut. Coaches love recommending
   tools to their audiences.
3. **Niche versions.** Duplicate and re-skin it for a specific audience — "Pricing Studio for Wedding
   Photographers," "…for Bookkeepers," "…for Hairstylists." Same file, targeted listing, new market.
4. **Raise the price** as reviews accumulate. Products with proof can charge more.

## Marketing content bank (steal these hooks)

- "You're not too expensive. You're too cheap. Here's the math."
- "I charged $45/hr for two years. This tool showed me I should've been at $91."
- "The exact number I plug in to price every client (in 5 minutes)."
- "Stop sending quotes in a text message. Do this instead." (show the Quote Generator)
- "How many clients do you *actually* need this month? Most people guess wrong."
- Before/after: a screenshot of the 'money left on the table' flag.

**Where to post:** Instagram Reels + carousels, TikTok, Pinterest, Threads, relevant Facebook
groups, LinkedIn (for consultants/VAs). Short video of the tool in action outperforms static posts.

## Money math (realistic, not hype)

| Sales / month | At $27 each | At $37 each |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | $270 | $370 |
| 50 | $1,350 | $1,850 |
| 100 | $2,700 | $3,700 |

Because there's no cost to deliver a digital download, that's almost all profit. And with Model B,
a single coaching client or pricing audit from your lead list can be worth more than 50 tool sales.
